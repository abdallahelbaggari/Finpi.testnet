1Who we are

FinPi (finpi.pi) is operated by ELBAGGARI INVESTMENT LIMITED, the controller of personal data processed by FinPi's servers. Contact: finpi.app@gmail.com, or Help & contact in the app.

2Information kept on your device

The following are stored in FinPi's storage on your device and are not sent to us: your currency, the name you add to receipts, your wallet address for balance display, budgets and categories, contacts and favourites, payment links, bill splits, payment reminders, price alerts, notifications, recent receipt checks, display settings (appearance, sounds, hidden amounts) and a short technical log of app errors.

Clearing FinPi's data in the Pi Browser removes them from your device. Keep your device locked, as anyone who can open it may see them.

4Information about other people

When you add customers or contacts, or create invoices, you may enter other people's names, Pi usernames, wallet addresses, phone numbers or email addresses. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so. Customers and invoices are included in your business records backup; contacts remain on your device.

5The Pi blockchain

Payments, deliveries and refunds are recorded on the public Pi blockchain, where wallet addresses, amounts, memos and times are visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us or by you. The narration you enter is shown to the recipient and kept in our transfer record; it is not written to the blockchain.

6Camera and images

The QR scanner and Verify receipt use your camera only while open. Images you select are read on your device to find a QR code and are not sent to us or stored. Logos you add to your business profile are resized on your device and kept with your business records.

7Service providers

  • Pi Network — authentication, payments, app-to-user payouts and the Pi blockchain.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, server functions and databases.
  • Google Fonts — fonts used to display the app.
  • Public market data providers — Pi prices and currency rates; no personal data is shared.

8What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • Use advertising trackers or build advertising profiles.
  • Request or store your passphrase, seed phrase or private key.
  • Move Pi from your wallet; only you can, by approving a payment through Pi Network.

9How we protect information

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) with strict browser security policies.
  • Secret keys held only on our servers as protected secrets.
  • Every payment verified with Pi Network before it is accepted.
  • Blockchain checks before any retry, so nothing is paid twice.
  • Receipts protected with server-signed verification codes.

No system is completely secure. If an incident affects your data, we will inform affected users in the app and act promptly to contain it.

11Your rights

You may request a copy of the data our servers hold about you, its correction or its deletion by contacting finpi.app@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. Records on the public Pi blockchain cannot be deleted.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU GDPR may give you further rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

12Children

FinPi is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used FinPi, contact us and we will delete their data.

13International processing

Our servers run on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed outside your country under industry-standard safeguards.

14Changes and contact

We update this policy when FinPi changes how it uses data and revise the effective date above. Material changes are announced in the app.
