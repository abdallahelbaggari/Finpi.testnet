1. Who we are

FinPi (finpi.pi) is an independent app for the Pi Browser, operated by its developer. Contact: finpi.app@gmail.com. FinPi is not run by Pi Network.

2. Kept only on your phone

These stay in your browser's storage on your device and are not sent to us: your receipts, budget and categories, notifications, recent payees, the name you add for receipts, your chosen currency, and the Pi wallet address you add to see your balance. Clearing FinPi's data in the Pi Browser deletes them. Anyone who can unlock your phone may see them, so keep it locked.

4. Pi sign-in and payments

When you sign in with Pi, the Pi app shares your Pi username and a user ID with FinPi. Payments happen in Pi's own payment window. We never see your passphrase, seed phrase, secret key or the balance in your Pi Wallet.

5. Transfers and the blockchain

Transfer records are used only by the software that runs the published transfer rules (see our Terms). We do not use them to profile you, and no automated or human decision about you is made beyond applying those same rules to everyone. The narration you type is shown to the recipient and kept in our transfer record; it is not written to the blockchain.

Payments, deliveries and refunds are written to the public Pi blockchain, where wallet addresses, amounts, Pi payment identifiers and times are visible to anyone. The payment description (for example "FinPi transfer from @username") is shown in the recipient's Pi Wallet and kept by Pi Network. Blockchain records are permanent and cannot be deleted by us or by you.

When you look up an address or verify a payment, your phone reads public data from the Pi blockchain (Pi Horizon) directly or through our server; we do not store what you look up.

6. Services we use

  • Pi Network: sign-in, payments, app-to-user payouts and the Pi blockchain.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, server functions and the database for transfers.
  • Public data services, which receive no personal data from us: OKX and CoinGecko (Pi price), open.er-api.com (currency rates) and Google Fonts (fonts).

7. What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or build advertising profiles.
  • We never ask for or store your passphrase, seed phrase or secret key.
  • We never move Pi from your wallet; only you can, by approving in Pi's window.

8. How we protect data

  • All connections use HTTPS, with browser security headers and a content policy that blocks scripts from unknown sites.
  • Secret keys live only on our server as protected secrets and are used only by the payment software.
  • Payments are checked with Pi Network before anything is approved; if a payment cannot be checked, it is not approved.
  • Before any retry the software checks the Pi blockchain, so nothing is ever paid twice.
  • Transfers have fixed limits and an overall daily cap, and the app warns before first-time payments.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will inform affected users in the app and act to contain it.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on your phone by clearing FinPi's data in the Pi Browser.
  • Write to finpi.app@gmail.com to ask for a copy of the data our server holds about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We reply within 30 days. Records on the public Pi blockchain cannot be deleted.

Depending on where you live (for example under Nigeria's Data Protection Act or the EU's GDPR), you may have further rights, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

11. Children

FinPi is only for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used FinPi, contact us and we will delete their data.

12. Where data is processed

Our server runs on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed in countries other than yours, with industry-standard security.

13. Changes and contact

We will update this page when FinPi changes how it uses data, and change the date at the top. Important changes are announced in the app. Questions: finpi.app@gmail.com.
