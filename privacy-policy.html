1Who we are

FinPi (finpi.pi) is operated by Elbaggari Investment Limited. We are responsible for the personal data processed by FinPi's server. Contact FinPi support or use Help & contact in the app.

2Information kept on your phone

The following are stored in FinPi's storage on your device and are not sent to us: your currency, the name you add for receipts, your wallet address for balance display, budgets and categories, contacts, payment links, bill splits, payment reminders, price alerts, notifications, recent receipt checks, display settings (sounds, hidden amounts) and a short technical log of app errors.

Clearing FinPi's data in the Pi Browser removes them from your phone. Keep your phone locked, as anyone who can open it may see them.

4Information about other people

When you add customers or contacts, or create invoices, you may enter other people's names, Pi usernames, wallet addresses, phone numbers or email addresses. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so. Customers and invoices are included in your business records backup; contacts stay on your phone.

5The Pi blockchain

Payments, deliveries and refunds are recorded on the public Pi blockchain, where wallet addresses, amounts, memos and times are visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us or by you. The narration you type is shown to the recipient and kept in our transfer record; it is not written to the blockchain.

6Camera and images

The QR scanner and Verify receipt use your camera only while open. Photos you choose from your gallery are read on your phone to find a QR code; images are not sent to us or stored. Logos you add to your business profile are resized on your phone and kept with your business records.

7Service providers

  • Pi Network — sign-in, payments, app-to-user payouts and the Pi blockchain.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, server functions and databases.
  • Google Fonts — fonts used to display the app.
  • Public market and exchange-rate data providers — Pi prices and currency rates; no personal data is shared with them.

8What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • Use advertising trackers or build advertising profiles.
  • Ask for or store your passphrase, seed phrase or secret key.
  • Move Pi from your wallet; only you can, by confirming a payment through Pi Network.

9How we protect information

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) with strict browser protections.
  • Secret keys kept only on our server as protected secrets.
  • Every payment checked with Pi Network before it is accepted.
  • Blockchain checks before any retry, so nothing is paid twice.
  • Receipts protected with server-signed verification codes.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform affected users in the app and act to contain it.

11Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data our server holds about you, its correction or its deletion by contacting FinPi support. We respond within 30 days. Records on the public Pi blockchain cannot be deleted.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU GDPR may give you further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

12Children

FinPi is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used FinPi, contact us and we will delete their data.

13International processing

Our server runs on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed outside your country with industry-standard safeguards.

14Changes and contact

We update this policy when FinPi changes how it uses data and change the effective date above. Material changes are announced in the app. Questions: contact FinPi support.
