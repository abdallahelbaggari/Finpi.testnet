Risk Disclosure

Effective 27 October 2026 · finpi.pi

Please read this before you send or receive Pi. FinPi makes Pi payments clearer and verifiable, but it cannot remove the risks of using a cryptocurrency. Only send what you can afford to lose, and only to people you trust.

1. Blockchain payments are final

Once a payment is recorded on the Pi blockchain it cannot be cancelled or reversed, by FinPi or anyone else. If you pay the wrong @username, the wrong amount, or someone who deceives you, FinPi cannot get your Pi back.

2. Scams

Scammers may pretend to be sellers, friends, Pi Network or FinPi staff. FinPi staff will never ask you to send Pi or to share your passphrase or secret key. The app warns you when you pay someone for the first time; take that warning seriously.

3. How transfers work, and what can go wrong

Your Pi goes to FinPi's app wallet and is passed on automatically to the recipient, normally within seconds. If Pi Network or the Pi blockchain is slow or unavailable, delivery can be delayed. If delivery is impossible, the software refunds you automatically under the published rules; the 0.01 π blockchain fee is not returned. Software can have faults; FinPi is designed to fail safely, so that when something cannot be confirmed, nothing is approved or paid.

4. Price and value

The value of Pi can rise or fall quickly, and prices shown by FinPi come from public market data that can be delayed or differ between sources. They are for information only and are not an offer to buy or sell, or financial advice. FinPi does not exchange Pi for other currencies.

5. Dependence on Pi Network

FinPi depends on Pi Network for sign-in, payments and the blockchain. Changes to Pi Network's rules, services or availability may limit or stop FinPi features.

6. Your device and account

Anyone who can unlock your phone or Pi account may be able to approve payments or see your receipts. Keep your phone locked and your passphrase private.

7. Business use

If you sell goods or services for Pi, you are responsible for your products, prices, taxes and any laws that apply. Disputes with customers are between you and them.

8. Regulation

Laws about cryptocurrencies differ by country and can change. You are responsible for checking that using Pi and FinPi is allowed where you live.
