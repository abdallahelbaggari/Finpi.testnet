1Blockchain payments are final

A payment recorded on the Pi blockchain cannot be cancelled or reversed by FinPi or anyone else. Pi sent to the wrong @username, the wrong wallet address or someone who deceives you cannot be recovered by FinPi.

2Fraud and impersonation

Fraudsters may pose as sellers, friends, Pi Network or FinPi staff. FinPi staff never ask you to send Pi or to share your passphrase or secret key. FinPi alerts you before a first payment to someone new and before a possible repeat payment.

3Receipts and documents

Images can be edited. Before you release goods or services, check any receipt with Verify receipt: the amount, transaction ID and verification code must match the blockchain result. An invoice or payment request is not proof of payment.

4Delivery and delays

Transfers depend on Pi Network and the Pi blockchain. If they are slow or unavailable, delivery can be delayed. If delivery is impossible, the software refunds you automatically under the published rules; the 0.01 π blockchain fee is not returned. FinPi is designed to fail safely: when a payment cannot be confirmed, nothing is paid.

5Price and value

The value of Pi can change quickly. Rates shown in FinPi come from public market data, may be delayed or differ between sources, and are for information only. FinPi does not exchange Pi for other currencies and gives no financial advice.

6Dependence on Pi Network

FinPi relies on Pi Network for sign-in, payments and the blockchain. Changes to Pi Network's rules, services or availability may limit or stop FinPi features.

7Your device and data

Anyone who can open your phone or Pi account may confirm payments or see your records. Keep your phone locked and your passphrase private. Data kept only on your phone is removed if you clear FinPi's data; signed-in users can restore their backed-up history and business records.

8Business use

If you sell for Pi, you are responsible for your products, prices, taxes and the laws that apply to you. Disputes with customers are between you and them.

9Regulation

Laws on digital assets differ by country and can change. You are responsible for confirming that using Pi and FinPi is permitted where you live. Questions: finpi.app@gmail.com.
