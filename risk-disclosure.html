1Blockchain payments are final

A payment recorded on the Pi blockchain cannot be cancelled or reversed by FinPi or anyone else. Pi sent to the wrong @username, the wrong wallet address or a person who deceives you cannot be recovered by FinPi.

2Fraud and impersonation

Fraudsters may pose as sellers, friends, Pi Network or FinPi staff. FinPi staff never ask you to send Pi or to share your passphrase or private key. FinPi alerts you before a first payment to a new recipient and before a possible duplicate payment.

3Receipts and documents

Images can be edited. Before releasing goods or services, check any receipt with Verify receipt: the amount, transaction ID and verification code must match the blockchain result. An invoice or payment request is not proof of payment.

4Settlement and delays

Transfers depend on Pi Network and the Pi blockchain. If either is slow or unavailable, delivery may be delayed. If delivery is impossible, you are refunded automatically under the published rules; the 0.01 π network fee is not returned. When a payment cannot be confirmed, nothing is paid.

5Price and value

The value of Pi can change quickly. Rates shown in FinPi come from public market data, may be delayed or vary between sources, and are for information only. FinPi does not exchange Pi for other currencies and does not provide financial advice.

6Dependence on Pi Network

FinPi relies on Pi Network for authentication, payments and the blockchain. Changes to Pi Network's rules, services or availability may limit or suspend FinPi features.

7Your device and data

Anyone who can open your device or Pi account may approve payments or view your records. Keep your device locked and your passphrase private. Data kept only on your device is removed if you clear FinPi's data; signed-in users can restore their backed-up history and business records.

8Business use

If you sell for Pi, you are responsible for your products, pricing, taxes and compliance. Disputes with customers are between you and them.

9Regulation

Laws on digital assets differ by country and may change. You are responsible for confirming that using Pi and FinPi is permitted where you live. Questions: finpi.app@gmail.com.
