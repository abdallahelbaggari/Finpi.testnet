7How transfers work

When you send Pi within FinPi, you approve the amount plus the 0.01 π network fee through Pi Network's user-to-app payment flow. Once Pi Network confirms the payment, FinPi's systems deliver the full amount to the recipient automatically: to a @username through Pi Network's app-to-user payment flow, or to a wallet address as a payment on the Pi blockchain. Funds pass through FinPi's app wallet only for the time settlement takes, normally a few seconds.

Every transfer ends in one of two outcomes, both recorded on the Pi blockchain: delivered to the recipient, or refunded to you.

Paying from your own Pi Wallet

You may prepare a payment in FinPi and complete it from your own Pi Wallet. FinPi then never receives your Pi; it reads the public Pi blockchain to identify the payment and issue your receipt.

8Transfer rules

Transfers are processed by software under fixed rules applied equally to every user. No individual at FinPi reviews, holds, freezes or reverses a transfer.

  • Before payment — a transfer is accepted only if the recipient is a FinPi user or a valid Pi wallet address with an active account, you are not paying yourself, the amount is at least 0.01 π and it is within any limits published in the app, and FinPi can settle it at that moment. Otherwise it is cancelled and you are not charged.
  • Delivery — the transfer is delivered once Pi Network confirms your payment. The Pi blockchain is checked before any retry, so a transfer is never paid twice.
  • Automatic refund — if the recipient can no longer receive, or delivery fails after repeated attempts over at least one hour, the full amount is refunded to your Pi Wallet. The 0.01 π network fee is not returned.
  • Finality — a delivered transfer is final and cannot be reversed by FinPi, the sender or the recipient.
  • Suspension — where Pi Network's rules, the law or a technical fault require it, new transfers may be paused for all users. Transfers already paid are still delivered or refunded.

Changes to these rules apply only to new transfers and are published in the app and in these Terms.

9Receipts, verification and statements

The public Pi blockchain is the final and authoritative record of every payment, delivery and refund. FinPi receipts show the transaction ID, a QR code and a verification code generated by FinPi's server from the payment's blockchain record. Statements carry a document ID and the transaction ID of every payment.

A receipt is genuine only when its amount, transaction ID and verification code match the result of Verify receipt. Where a receipt or statement differs from the blockchain, the blockchain prevails.

Creating, altering or presenting a false FinPi receipt, statement or invoice is prohibited and may be reported to the competent authorities.
