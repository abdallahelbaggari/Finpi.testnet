6. Sending and receiving Pi

  • How it works: you pay the amount plus a 0.01 π blockchain fee to FinPi's app wallet in Pi's payment window. As soon as Pi Network confirms your payment, FinPi's software automatically pays the full amount from the app wallet to the recipient: to a @username through Pi Network's official app-to-user payments, and to a wallet address as a payment signed by the app wallet on the Pi blockchain. Your Pi passes through the app wallet only for the time this takes, normally seconds.
  • No balances, no custody: each transfer ends in one of only two ways, both on the Pi blockchain: delivered to the recipient, or refunded to you.
  • Wallet addresses: FinPi checks that the address is valid and has a Pi account before you pay. You must make sure it belongs to the person you mean to pay; Pi sent to a wrong address cannot be recovered.
  • Check before you send: the app shows a final check screen with the recipient, amount, fee and total, and warns you when you pay someone for the first time. Make sure everything is right before you approve.
  • Receiving: others send to your FinPi @username, or to your Pi wallet address from Pi Wallet. Nothing is paid until the sender approves in Pi's window.

Paying from your own Pi Wallet

You can also fill in a payment in FinPi and pay it from your own Pi Wallet. In that case FinPi never receives or holds your Pi: you send it directly to the recipient's address, and FinPi only reads the public Pi blockchain to find the payment and create your receipt. The memo FinPi gives you helps match the payment. You can also create a receipt for any Pi payment from its transaction ID.

7. The transfer rules

Transfers are handled only by software following these fixed rules, applied the same way to everyone. No person at FinPi reviews, approves, holds, freezes, reverses or decides on any individual transfer or account.

  • Before you pay, a transfer is accepted only if: the recipient has signed in to FinPi with Pi, or is a valid Pi wallet address with a Pi account; you are not sending to yourself; the amount is at least 0.01 π and within the per-transfer limit; and it fits within the daily limits per person and the app's overall daily limit. The current limits are shown in the app. If a rule is not met, the transfer is refused or cancelled in Pi's window and you are not charged.
  • After you pay, the software delivers the transfer once Pi Network confirms your payment. Before any retry it checks the Pi blockchain, so a transfer is never paid twice.
  • Automatic refund: if the recipient can no longer receive (for example, the address has no Pi account), or delivery still fails after repeated attempts over at least one hour, the software refunds the full amount you sent to your Pi wallet on the Pi blockchain. The 0.01 π fee is not returned, because it pays the blockchain fee. You get a refund receipt and a notification.
  • Once delivered, a transfer is final. FinPi cannot and will not reverse it, including at the request of the sender or the recipient.
  • Pausing: if Pi Network's rules, the law or a technical fault require it, new transfers may be paused for all users at once; the app shows this. A pause never applies to one person, and transfers already paid are still delivered or refunded automatically under these rules.
  • If the rules or limits change, the change applies only to new transfers and is shown in the app and in these terms.
