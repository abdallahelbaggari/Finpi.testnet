6How transfers work

When you send Pi inside FinPi, you confirm the amount plus the 0.01 π blockchain fee through Pi Network. Once Pi Network confirms the payment, FinPi's software delivers the full amount to the recipient automatically: to a @username through Pi Network's app-to-user payments, or to a wallet address as a payment on the Pi blockchain. The Pi passes through FinPi's app wallet only for the time this takes, normally seconds.

Every transfer ends in one of two ways, both recorded on the Pi blockchain: delivered to the recipient, or refunded to you.

Paying from your own Pi Wallet

You may prepare a payment in FinPi and pay it from your own Pi Wallet. FinPi then never receives your Pi: it reads the public Pi blockchain to find the payment and create your receipt.

7Transfer rules

Transfers are handled by software under fixed rules applied equally to everyone. No person at FinPi reviews, holds, freezes or reverses an individual transfer.

  • Before payment — a transfer is accepted only if the recipient has used FinPi or is a valid Pi wallet address with a Pi account, you are not paying yourself, the amount is at least 0.01 π, and it fits any limits published in the app. Otherwise it is cancelled and you are not charged.
  • Delivery — the software delivers the transfer once Pi Network confirms your payment, and checks the Pi blockchain before any retry so a transfer is never paid twice.
  • Automatic refund — if the recipient can no longer receive, or delivery fails after repeated attempts over at least one hour, the full amount is refunded to your Pi wallet. The 0.01 π blockchain fee is not returned.
  • Finality — a delivered transfer is final and cannot be reversed by FinPi, the sender or the recipient.
  • Pausing — if Pi Network's rules, the law or a technical fault require it, new transfers may be paused for all users at once. Transfers already paid are still delivered or refunded.

Changes to these rules apply only to new transfers and are published in the app and in these Terms.

8Receipts, verification and statements

The public Pi blockchain is the final and authoritative record of every payment, delivery and refund. FinPi receipts show the transaction ID, a QR code and a verification code generated by FinPi's server from the payment's blockchain record. Statements carry a document ID and the transaction ID of every payment.

A receipt is genuine only when its amount, transaction ID and verification code match the result of Verify receipt or FinPi's verify page. If a receipt or statement differs from the blockchain, the blockchain prevails.

Creating, altering or presenting a false FinPi receipt, statement or invoice is prohibited and may be reported to the authorities.
